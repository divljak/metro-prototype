Metro Bank is an award-winning independent UK bank offering corporate, commercial and SME banking and specialist mortgage lending, alongside retail and private banking services. 2. # **Objectives**

   1. # Core Objectives

The core objective is to develop a comprehensive UI/UX ecosystem and own the delivery iterations though established product management and development principles that serves as an end-to-end lifecycle interface to the banking ecosystem.

The solution must serve diverse customer and user groups across SME, Commercial, Corporate, branch users, agents and relationship managers.   Consideration should also be given to how this solution could eventually service Retail and Private Banking segments in the future.

Our vision is to differentiate Metro through a seamless, intuitive and innovative digital ecosystem that spans mobile, web and in-store environments, delivering a consistent, personalized experience. 

Our goal is to identify a development partner that will collaborate with Metro engineering teams and our partners to design and build a ‘NextGen’ channels and engagement layer. 

The partner will be responsible for design and delivery of UI/UX, journey orchestration, front-end components ultimately transferring capability and ownership to Metro Bank teams.

The selected supplier must:

* Establish shared design principles prioritizing simplicity, trust, accessibility, and proactive guidance across channels; these principles will anchor decision-making and ensure consistency across journeys

* Provide a modular design system and a component library which can seamlessly integrate across multiple channels and interactions

* Deliver production-grade, secure channel layer for web and mobile considering Mobile-First design pattern (and PWA if proposed) ensuring the new channel layers integrate with the Metro Bank IDAM and back-end systems, and utilising 3rd party SDKs as agreed with Metro Bank

* Implement and/or integrate to proposed journey orchestration with personalization and state handling

* Provide flexibility, scalability, differentiation and innovation powered by AI while ensuring explainability, user control, and consent for AI-driven experiences

* Achieve measurable UX and performance targets (e.g., KPIs per journey could include: time to complete, drop-off rate, error rate, accessibility score, CSAT, active usage); integrate analytics (and crashlytics) to instrument journeys and deliver measurable improvement

* Ensure Metro retains full ownership of intellectual property (IP) related to solution design, code, workflows and artifacts delivered under this engagement

* Build-operate-transfer knowledge and operational ownership to Metro Bank within agreed timeframe.

ASK: Supplier to provide overview of their test strategy and automation approach

Our test strategy is built on a "shift-left" philosophy, embedding quality assurance throughout the development lifecycle. Our automation approach will include:

\- Definitions of Quality: Clear “Definition of Ready” and “Definition of Done” will apply to all work items, ensuring they meet agreed business, security, and compliance criteria.  
\- Testing Coverage: Testing will include functional, integration, security, performance, and accessibility.  
\- Acceptance Criteria: Critical user flows (account creation, order, reorder) must work without defects; the system must pass compliance and security checks before release.  
\- Evidence: Test reports and risk acceptance documentation will be shared with Client stakeholders.

  \* **Unit and Integration Tests:** Utilizing Jest/React Testing Library for comprehensive testing of individual React/ReactNative components and their interactions.  
  \* **End-to-End (E2E) Tests:** Implementing Cypress for critical user journeys across web and mobile, ensuring seamless functionality and user experience.  
  \* **Visual Regression Testing:** Automated tools will be integrated into our pipeline to maintain design consistency across all channels, validating design tokens, components, and layouts.  
  \* **Performance Testing:** We will incorporate tools like Lighthouse/WebPageTest within our CI/CD to continuously monitor and validate frontend performance against defined FCP, LCP, and TTI budgets.  
  \* **Accessibility Testing:** Automated WCAG 2.2 AA checks will be a standard part of our pipeline, supplemented by expert manual audits and assistive technology testing.  
  \* **Contract Testing:** We will collaborate with Infosys to implement contract testing, ensuring strict adherence to API contracts and supporting a consumer-driven contract approach for robust integration.

This strategy ensures a high-quality, resilient, and performant frontend delivery.
